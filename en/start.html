A Plurality Atlas

A map of the book Plurality and the ideas, mechanisms, practices, people and debates around it, one "What is…?" question at a time. Written for first-time readers; every article ends with links to the book, talks and other primary sources.

Four articles to read first.

What is ⿻ Plurality?Technology — and a way of thinking about it — that helps people with different views and backgrounds work together instead of clashing. Audrey Tang of Taiwan and the economist Glen Weyl define it in their book of the same name as "technology for collaboration across social difference".What is Broad Listening?Technology and practice that uses AI to read thousands or tens of thousands of free-form comments, group similar opinions, and turn them into a "map of issues", so that one person can listen to many. It is the reverse of broadcasting on TV or radio.What is the 6-Pack of Care?Six principles for checking whether AI actually serves people — not only by looking inside the AI, but by asking whether people on the ground were heard, who is accountable, whether failures can be repaired, and whether it can be switched off. Audrey Tang and Caroline Green set them out in 2025.What is Reverse Alignment?The other side of "alignment", which fits AI to humans: an effort to rebuild society's institutions, rules, people's skills and the ways decisions are made so that AI can be used safely and its benefits widely shared. Glen Weyl and others put it forward as an agenda in 2026.
